Our Story

Coming home should mean somewhere to land.

Legacy 44 provides safe transitional housing, peer support, workforce development, life-skills education, and supportive services that empower justice-impacted individuals to successfully reintegrate into their communities while reducing recidivism.

Leadership

Meet our founder

Yaw Harrison

Founder & Executive Director

Yaw Harrison is the Founder and Executive Director of Legacy 44, a North Carolina nonprofit he built to transform lived experience with incarceration into a mission-driven pathway of stability, opportunity, and lasting change for returning citizens.

A graduate of North Carolina A&T State University with a B.A. in Political Science, Yaw is also a North Carolina Certified Peer Support Specialist. His lived experience, professional background, and commitment to service became the foundation for Legacy 44.

Legacy 44 was formed to help bridge the gap between incarceration and independence by providing returning citizens with access to safe transitional housing, peer support, employment and life-skills development, community resources, and other supportive services. The organization is built on the belief that a person's past does not have to determine their future.

As Founder and Executive Director, Yaw leads Legacy 44's mission to transform lived experience into meaningful service—helping returning citizens rebuild stability, restore hope, strengthen their families, and create a lasting legacy beyond incarceration.

Our approach

Housing first. Everything else follows from there.

Stable housing

Safe, sober housing with no arbitrary time limit — residents stay as long as they need to get on their feet.

Case management

One-on-one support connecting residents to employment, healthcare, and family reunification services.

Community

Regular events and mentorship so residents build a support network that outlasts their stay.

Legacy 44

Transitional housing and reentry support, built by and for the local community.
