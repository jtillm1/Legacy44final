Our Story

Coming home should mean somewhere to land.

Legacy 44 provides safe transitional housing, peer support, workforce development, life-skills education, and supportive services that empower justice-impacted individuals to successfully reintegrate into their communities while reducing recidivism.

How we got here

Our path so far

  1. 01

    2022 — Founded around one house

    Started with a single rented home and four beds, run entirely by volunteers.

  2. 02

    2023 — Added case management

    Brought on our first part-time case manager to help residents with jobs, ID recovery, and healthcare enrollment.

  3. 03

    2024 — Expanded to three properties

    Community donations and a city partnership let us open two additional homes.

  4. 04

    Today — Six homes, growing waitlist

    We're working to open a seventh home and build a formal mentorship program.

Our approach

Housing first. Everything else follows from there.

Stable housing

Safe, sober housing with no arbitrary time limit — residents stay as long as they need to get on their feet.

Case management

One-on-one support connecting residents to employment, healthcare, and family reunification services.

Community

Regular events and mentorship so residents build a support network that outlasts their stay.

Legacy 44

Transitional housing and reentry support, built by and for the local community.
